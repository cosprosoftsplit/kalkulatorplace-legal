Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?

Open the calculator tab, enter either a gross or net amount, choose a verified local tax profile or custom rates, and review the net salary, employer cost, and detailed breakdown. You can then save or share the result.

What premium features are available?

The premium purchase is a one-time ad removal upgrade. Core salary calculation, comparison, history, and sharing remain available in the free version. Once purchased, the ad-free upgrade is yours permanently for the same Apple ID.

How do I restore my purchase?

If you have previously purchased a premium upgrade and need to restore it (for example, after reinstalling the App or switching devices), open the App and tap "Restore Purchases" in the settings or premium section. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase. The restoration process is handled by Apple and requires an active internet connection.

How do I request a refund?

All purchases are processed by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID. Select the purchase and follow the instructions to submit your refund request.

Is my data private?

Your saved calculations stay on your device. The free version may use Google's consent and advertising frameworks after privacy consent, and Apple handles purchases. Please read the Privacy Policy for the exact breakdown of what we control versus what third parties may process.

Are the calculations official?

No. The app provides informed estimates based on published Croatian rules and the selected local rates, but it does not replace your employer's payroll calculation, an accountant, or formal tax advice.

Why might I not see ads?

Ads may be unavailable if you are using the ad-free upgrade, if privacy consent does not allow ads, or if the advertising SDK has not loaded an ad yet. Missing ads are expected in some situations and do not affect calculation features.
